Product Manager: Cover Letter & CV

Dear talent team,

I am writing to express my interest in exploring a partnership within your organization and offer my expertise in Product Management. I am delighted to share my CV with you for your consideration.

With over five years of experience in Product Management, I have proven competence in enabling teams to accomplish their objectives by developing innovative solutions that combine profound customer understanding, powerful technology and strategic business needs.

As a Senior Product Manager at Cansativa, a leader in medical cannabis distribution in Germany, I led the initiative of connecting pharmacies, doctors, patients and producers via an online marketplace and a mobile application. Central to my role was developing a compelling positioning based on product strategy, competitive analysis, a clear UX/UI direction and keen customer empathy.

I’ve also operated as Product Owner and UX/UI Designer at Innosabi, a leader in innovation management tools based in Munich, where key feature requests were crafted by means of cross-functional collaboration, alongside internal teams and international clients to guarantee alignment with their company goals and overarching strategy.

Additionally, I’ve had the opportunity of working at CKSoul, a wellness and lifestyle brand in Los Angeles, as Head of Product & Manufacturing, where I collaborated in launch strategies alongside the marketing, sales and customer success teams and ensured the successful production of medical-grade, customer-centric products.

I am confident I could be of great contribution to your mission. I’m eager to learn more about your mission and look forward to your reply.
